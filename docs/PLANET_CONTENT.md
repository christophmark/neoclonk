# Planet content and community collections

The published Planet library contains **53 official scenarios**, extracted from the publisher’s Clonk Planet 4.65 freeware release. Original packed groups are unchanged. `planet-content/provenance/planet-release.json` records the publisher URL, installer checksum and each extracted file hash. Extraction verifies the installer’s file checksums without executing its Windows program.

The publisher’s original content terms apply: CC BY-NC 4.0, with attribution to RedWolf Design / Matthes Bender and credited contributors. Engine code has separate ISC terms. The Clonk trademark notice and original credits remain in the distribution.

## Community inclusion rule

Community submissions are published only when their terms clearly permit this distribution without obtaining special author permission. Uploading a collection for evaluation does not override individual author notices. Archives with unresolved terms are not included in the downloadable game, preview gallery or public source mirror.

The supplied contest collection contains 310 selectable scenarios across Planet, Golden Wipf Edition, Endeavor and Rage generations. No general third-party redistribution grant was found in the collection. The original contest rules located in it promise publication on RedWolf’s site; they do not grant a general right for other sites to rehost submissions. Some object notices also require author permission for reuse or modification. Those scope-specific restrictions are not treated as a blanket prohibition on playing an original archive, but neither establish redistribution permission.

The current public library therefore includes the official Planet release and the existing official Rage release/add-ons. It does not include the unresolved contest collection. Its files and previews are excluded by the staging and source-export allowlists. Additional community packs can be added when a clear license or other redistribution grant is documented.
