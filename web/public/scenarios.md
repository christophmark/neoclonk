## Official add-on packs

The publisher lists four additional packs on its Clonk Rage release page:

- **Knights:** knights, horses, weapons and castle construction.
- **Far Worlds:** Arctic, Jungle and Deep Sea.
- **Fantasy:** mages, dragons and extended magic.
- **Western:** Wild West characters, towns and equipment.

Other community packs exist; they are not part of this base-release inventory.
